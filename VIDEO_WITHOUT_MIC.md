# How to record without a PC microphone (Windows)

1. Start the app with `START_WINDOWS.bat`.
2. In Chrome, sign in as the demo administrator.
3. Press `Win + G` to open Xbox Game Bar. Under Capture, check that microphone capture is off.
4. Use `Win + Alt + R` to start and stop screen recording (when Game Bar supports the window).
5. Demonstrate login, dashboard, ticket filters, assignment, notes and audit trail. Follow `DEMO_SCRIPT_EN.md`.
6. Export the recording and add English captions. Use a mobile device to record your own voice if speech is required.
7. Check that any demonstration contains **only fictitious sample data**.
8. Upload the finished file to YouTube as **Unlisted**, only after reviewing content and permission requirements.

Optional: edit the video in Clipchamp or a video editor of your choice. For the EgoTECHWORLD application, the primary demonstration should be your **thesis system**, if that is what their assignment expects. This CasePilot script is for a separate portfolio sample.
